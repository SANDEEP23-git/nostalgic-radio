FAQ

How does the radio work?

Select a music mode and the corresponding playlist will play.

Where does the music come from?

Music is played through YouTube's embedded player.

Can I change the appearance?

Yes. Open Settings and choose Glass, Solid or Auto.

Can I turn the rain off?

Yes. Use the rain button in the top-right corner.

What is Screensaver mode?

Screensaver mode creates a distraction-free cinematic view for listening.

Can I play music in the background or with the phone screen off?

Yes! Nostalgic Radio supports seamless background and screen-off playback. When you switch to another app or turn off/lock your phone screen, music continues playing with active lock screen media controls.
